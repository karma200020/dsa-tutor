# FAANG Interview Tutor Plan

## Goal
Build a realistic, easy-to-understand SWE interview tutor for engineers with 3–5 years of experience. It must cover the breadth of DSA, LLD, and HLD expected in FAANG-style interviews, while still teaching with simple explanations, examples, diagrams, and patterns.

## Curriculum scope
- DSA foundations: arrays, strings, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, stacks, queues, binary search, linked lists, trees, heaps, graphs, DP, greedy, backtracking.
- LLD: OOP, design patterns, SOLID, class modeling, APIs, interfaces, extensibility, common design exercises.
- HLD: system design foundations, load balancing, caching, storage, SQL vs NoSQL, consistency, queues, CDN, estimation, scaling tradeoffs, and full design exercises.
- Interview simulation: communication, complexity articulation, tradeoff explanation, and timed rounds.

## Teaching strategy
1. Explain the intuition in plain English first.
2. Show a canonical example and an interview-style walkthrough.
3. Give a brute-force idea and explain why it fails.
4. Introduce the optimal pattern and the invariant.
5. Add code examples, edge cases, and complexity analysis.
6. Finish with tips, mistakes, and practice problems.

## Coverage guardrails
- Ensure every major topic family has multiple lessons, not just a single placeholder.
- Keep DSA, LLD, and HLD each rich enough for actual interview prep.
- Use a module-based syllabus with real topic coverage, not shallow summaries.
- Prioritize breadth, depth, and clear explanations over cosmetic design.
